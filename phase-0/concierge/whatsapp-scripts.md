# WhatsApp scripts

Consistent wording so twenty tasks are comparable. Adapt the tone, never the substance — the
price, the deadline and the safety line stay exactly as written.

## Intake, first contact

> Hi [name] — you mentioned [thing]. I'm running a small pilot this month where I get things like
> that done for people. Send it to me in your own words, however messy, and I'll tell you what it
> costs and when you'll have it. No obligation if the price doesn't work for you.

**Do not** send a menu of categories. What they ask for unprompted is the data ([A7](../assumptions.md)).

## Scoping, back to the requester

> Here's what I've understood: [restatement in one or two sentences].
> You'll get: [the specific deliverable — three options with prices, a claim number, a filled form].
> By: [day and time].
> Cost: ₹[X], paid by UPI after you've seen it.
> Shall I start?

Restating the task in your own words catches the misunderstanding before it costs a day. If the
restatement is wrong, that is a finding for the deliverable template.

## Briefing the doer

> Task: [restatement]
> Deliverable: [exact fields required]
> Deadline: [day and time]
> You'll be paid: ₹[Y] within 24 hours of the requester approving it. That's after an 18% platform
> fee on ₹[X].
> Two rules: never ask them for a password, OTP or PIN. On any call, say you're calling on behalf
> of [name], who has authorised you — never that you are them.
> Can you take it?

State the fee every time. [A6](../assumptions.md) is only testable if they hear the real number
before they say yes.

## Delivering

> Here's [task]: [the deliverable].
> [If relevant: the reference number / booking link / photo.]
> Anything missing, tell me and I'll get it fixed today.

## Payment request

> All good? If so, ₹[X] to [UPI ID]. And two quick questions, 30 seconds:
> 1. Did you use it, or did you end up doing it over yourself?
> 2. What's the next thing you'd send me?

## Declining a task

> I can't take this one — [reason: it needs your account password / it needs a licensed
> professional / it's outside what I can do safely]. What I *can* do is [alternative], if that's
> useful.

Log every decline with the reason. Declines map directly to FR-8's prohibited-content rules, and
a rule written from a real decline is better than one written from imagination.

## Asking for a repeat

At the end of a successful task, once:

> If something like this comes up again, just send it over. Same number.

Then say nothing more. Whether they come back unprompted is the closest thing Phase 0 has to a
retention measurement.
