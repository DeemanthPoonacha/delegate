# Screener

Five questions, two minutes, before booking anyone for an interview. Ask them over WhatsApp or a
call. The point is to avoid spending 45 minutes on someone who will be polite and useless.

## Requester screener

1. What did you do last weekend that you would rather not have done yourself?
2. Roughly what is your monthly household income? _(band is fine: under ₹50k / ₹50k–1.5L / above ₹1.5L)_
3. Have you ever paid someone outside your household to do something administrative for you —
   an agent, a CA, a broker, a maid, a consultant? What did you pay?
4. When did you last spend more than an hour on hold, in a queue, or comparing options online?
5. Do you use UPI daily?

**Book them if:** Q1 produces a concrete recent example, Q3 is a yes with a number, and Q4 was
within the last month. Income band matters less than a demonstrated history of paying to offload
work — someone who has never paid for help is a hypothesis, not a customer.

**Do not book:** anyone who says "I'd definitely use that" before you have described anything.

## Doer screener

1. How many free hours do you have in a typical weekday, and when are they?
2. What have you earned money from in the last six months?
3. What is the lowest hourly rate you would accept for work you can do from your phone at home?
4. Do you have a PAN card and a bank account in your own name?
5. Have you worked on any gig or freelance platform before? Which, and why did you stop?

**Book them if:** Q1 gives 3+ hours in a predictable block, Q3 is at or below ₹300, and Q4 is yes.
Q4 is not optional — a doer without PAN and a bank account cannot be paid under
[KYC-2](../../docs/compliance/README.md) and is not a real supply-side participant.

**Flag for the concierge run:** anyone whose Q2 shows domain expertise — an ex-banker, an
insurance agent, a teacher. They are the Meera and Faisal personas and they are the ones who can
charge more than a generalist.

## Recruiting targets

| Segment | Target | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Salaried professionals, 28–40 | 12 | Tech-park WhatsApp groups, apartment association groups, gyms |
| Founders and small business owners | 8 | Co-working spaces, local founder groups |
| 55+, low digital comfort | 5 | Parents and their friends; senior-citizen associations |
| NRI with parents in India | 5 | Personal networks; NRI community groups |
| Students | 12 | College noticeboards, campus groups |
| Homemakers with prior careers | 10 | Apartment groups, returnship communities |
| Between-jobs graduates | 8 | Job-seeker groups, alumni networks |

Recruit from at least four distinct sources per side. A sample drawn entirely from one WhatsApp
group is one opinion held by thirty people.
